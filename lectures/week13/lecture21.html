Estimating home-field advantage

What is home-field advantage?

  • Fact (?): home teams have an advantage over away teams.
  • How to quantify it?
  • How to estimate it from data?

NFL data

What does home field advantage mean in terms of these data?

Margin

  • The margin of victory is defined as:

    \[ \text{margin} = \text{home team score} - \text{away team score} \]

  • What’s the average margin of victory for home teams in the NFL?

Controlling for team strength

  • Home-field advantage may be confounded by team strength.

  • Strong teams tend to win more often, and if strong teams happen to play more home games, that could bias our estimate of home-field advantage.

  • To control for team strength we can add team-specific fixed effects:

    \[ \text{margin}_i = \text{HFA} + \text{Strength}_{\text{home},i} - \text{Strength}_{\text{away},i} + \epsilon_i \]

A model for team strengths

  • Should we have that each team has a unique strength?
  • We want a regression model that:
    • has one strength number for each team,
    • adds that number when the team is playing at home,
    • subtracts that number when the team is playing away.
  • Does the model we just fit do that?

Assigning a unique strength to each team

  • Build new columns in our model for each team:
  • In the column for Team A:
    • Put +1 if Team A is the home team in that game
    • Put −1 if Team A is the away team
    • Put 0 otherwise

The revised model

\[ \text{margin}_i = \text{HFA} + \text{home team strength}_i - \text{away team strength}_i + \epsilon_i \]
